About & method

Touchline is a season journal for NCAA Division II men's soccer, following the Great American Conference, Lone Star Conference and Gulf South Conference. It is independent and unofficial: it has no connection to any conference, programme or governing body. Everything on it comes from the programmes' own published pages — and what they did not publish is shown as what it is.

WHAT THE WORDS MEAN

PLAYED

A match marked final whose score was published. Touchline counts nothing it cannot show.

SILENT FINAL

A match a programme's site marked finished without ever publishing a score. The silence is the site's own; it stays on the record, named, and every collect asks again.

GAP

A match whose result Touchline holds but whose detail — the box score behind it — the collector could not reach. Never a friendly, never a silent final: a gap is the one absence nothing else on the page names.

FRIENDLY

A match played outside the record everywhere: no wins, no goals, no counts. Named where it appears, counted nowhere. The 2026 collection is the first to mark them, so on earlier seasons Touchline cannot tell a friendly from a match that counted — and does not guess which was which.

HOW MUCH IS KNOWN

Every claim on this site wears a mark saying how far it is from the published page it came from. The marks are fixed per kind of claim, never chosen to suit one.

OBSERVED ●●●
a fact a programme published, as collected.
DERIVED ●●○
computed exactly from published values — the arithmetic is shown.
SIGNAL ●●○
a pattern in the data whose cause is unverified.
PROJECTED ●○○
forward-looking; the season will answer it.
CONTEXT
biography, not a finding.

Where a claim carries figures, the chip opens onto them: the numbers the sentence rests on, and the layer they were read from.

WHAT IS CHECKED

Every finding is recomputed against the collected data before publish — anything unverifiable is dropped, not softened, and every number in prose must answer to a figure that was checked.

WHERE IT COMES FROM

A collector reads each programme's own schedule, roster, statistics and box-score pages, once a day, and writes what it finds to a file. This site is built from those files and one hand-kept reference — each programme's nickname and city, the only facts here no collector produced. No estimates, no third-party feeds.
